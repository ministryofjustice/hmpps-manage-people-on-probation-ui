### New information in the MPOP header

You can now view important information about a person on probation at the top of the page while using MPOP.

The new header shows:

- the person on probation’s name, CRN, date of birth and tier
- risk flags from NDelius
- risk information from OASys and ARNS
- a status tag showing whether they’re in custody, in breach or deceased
- the probation practitioner managing the case

You can select an NDelius risk flag, their tier or ‘Managed by’ to view more detailed information.

When adding an appointment or contact, you can hide the NDelius risk flags to help you focus on the task.

### Improved CRISS structure for appointment notes

You can now add appointment notes using either a single text box or the CRISS format.

If you use CRISS, individual text boxes for each section help you organise notes under clear headings such as ‘Check in’. You can switch between the two formats at any time.

This helps you structure your notes more easily.

### ‘Prevent concerns’ flag

We now show when a case with a supervision package has a ‘Prevent concerns’ flag. New guidance explains that a person on probation can receive additional appointments while the flag is active. This means the usual appointment limit no longer applies.
