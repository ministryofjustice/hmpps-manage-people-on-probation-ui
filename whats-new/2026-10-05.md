### View supervision package information

You can now see a summary of a person on probation’s supervision package on the Overview and Appointment pages. This visualises key information relating to the Independent Sentencing Review (ISR) from 1 October 2026.

Clear, contextual information helps you understand appointment expectations and see where someone is within their sentence lifecycle.

You can view:

- eligibility for a supervision package
- tier, including provisional or missing tier
- appointment progress, including the number of remaining supervision package appointments
- current supervision stage
- updated information when circumstances change

The service also displays variations such as:

- breach or recall
- additional discretionary appointments for women
- Integrated Offender Management (IOM)
- Lifer, extended determinate sentence, and Imprisonment for Public Protection (IPP) cases
- offender personality disorder (OPD) treatment

### Upload JPEGs

You can now upload JPEG files (up to 5MB) when you create non-appointment contacts. You can also upload them when editing any contact type, including appointments that have already been created.

### Confirm outcomes and arrange the next appointment more easily

Users said it was confusing to arrange the next appointment while logging an appointment outcome. We’ve improved labels and reduced some screens to make the process clearer.

You can also now record an enforcement outcome and arrange the next appointment within the same journey, helping you complete both actions together.

### Manage pre-sentence appointments on NDelius

We do not fully support the range of appointment types and non-enforceable outcomes required for pre-sentence activity. For that reason, we’ve removed pre-sentence appointments from appointment creation and outcome logging.

This means you can manage pre-sentence events on NDelius without moving across systems.
