### Add and update 100 more contact types

You can now create and manage 100 more contact types that you regularly use. This makes it easier to manage your contacts in one place.

You can:

- add 22 contacts with outcomes
- search for contacts using categories or keyword search
- update contacts created in Manage people on probation, for example adding notes, uploading files or changing the outcome
- choose from 115 contact types in total, including the ones already available

### Enter date and times more easily

To make it easier for you to arrange appointments, we’ve improved how you enter dates and times.

You can enter:

- dates and times using spaces, dashes, slashes or full stops
- dates and times without leading zeros, for example 1/1/2026 or 9:30
- shortened years, for example 26 instead of 2026

### See updated risk predictors

We’ve updated the risk predictor screen badges. If the person on probation has an assessment created after 23 February 2026, you’ll see the new risk predictors in the header.

The names describe the type of reoffending they predict. They are spelled out, to avoid acronyms and make them simpler to scan.

### View updated criminogenic needs

We’ve updated the criminogenic needs labels on the risk and overview pages to improve clarity and align with OASys criminogenic needs.

### See more details when you search

When you search for a person on probation, you’ll now see their allocated probation practitioner and probation delivery unit (PDU).

### Bug fixes

We’ve fixed bugs:

- on the Manage appointment screen, so you can now arrange another appointment from this screen, even if one is already booked
- that caused security errors and stopped you adding notes

Thanks to everyone who reported these issues.
