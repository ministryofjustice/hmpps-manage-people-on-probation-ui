### Send text message appointment confirmations

You can now choose to send a text message confirmation of an appointment to a person on probation. The text is automatically sent after you’ve arranged the appointment and is added to the contact log.

Text messages will show:

- appointment time and date
- practitioner first name
- appointment type, for example office or telephone
- fixed content that cannot be edited

Texts are sent by the GOV.UK Notify service and will show as coming from GOVUK.

People on probation based at Welsh probation delivery units will get Welsh and English texts if their preferred language is recorded as Welsh on NDelius.

Appointment locations are not currently included.

To text a person on probation, they need a mobile number they have agreed can be used for text messages. If there is no number, check whether they have one.

To make text message statuses clear, we’ll automatically add updates depending on the message status. You’ll see if:

- the message was successfully sent
- the message was not delivered

### New overview page

You can now see an improved overview of a person on probation in MPOP. This helps you quickly understand key information about them.

The new overview page shows:

- if they’re a risk to staff
- sentence information, including sentence date, length, main offence and order
- risk and criminogenic needs, including information from OASys and ARNS, and flags from NDelius
- addresses, disabilities and personal circumstances

It also shows throughcare information, including:

- prison offender manager (POM)
- current or last prison
- responsible officer handover date (if relevant)
- release date

Each section includes a link to view the full information in MPOP.

### See outcomes to log from the last 3 months

To help you manage your outcomes more efficiently, we now only show appointments that need an outcome from the last 3 months. This helps you focus on recent outstanding outcomes.

### See appointments you’re attending on your homepage

If you attend an appointment with a person on probation but are not their responsible officer, you can now see the appointment on your MPOP homepage.

These appointments appear in your ‘Upcoming appointments’ list before they take place and in your ‘Outcomes to log’ list afterwards.
